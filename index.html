CLIENT QUICK START

Let’s get your
project started.

To help me provide an accurate estimate, please prepare the following details. They don’t have to be final—even a rough idea is a good start.

01

Business or project name
What does your business do, and who would you like to reach?

02

Website goals
What would you like visitors to do—send a message, book a service, or find information?

03

Pages and features
For example: Home, About, Services, a gallery, contact form, login, or dashboard.

04

Copy, photos, and logo
Gather your content and brand assets. If they’re not ready yet, let me know so we can discuss options.

05

Design inspiration
Share two or three website links or screenshots that reflect the style you like.

06

Budget and deadline
Share your target budget and when you need the project completed. Clear expectations help us plan.

07

Domain and hosting
Do you already have a domain? Never send passwords—if access is needed, we’ll discuss a secure way to provide it.
